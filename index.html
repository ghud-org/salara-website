what we do

Strategy first, then the steady work of showing up.

Most engagements combine the first four as one monthly plan. Website support is available as an add-on.

  1. 01

    Account audit & strategy

    A full review of every account you run, followed by a strategy presentation: which platforms are worth your time, what each one is for, and how they fit together under one plan.

  2. 02

    Content planning & creation

    A content calendar and the posts to fill it: reels, feed posts, and stories. The visual direction comes from your own photography, with clean template-based design, so everything looks like it belongs together.

  3. 03

    Story-first engagement

    Stay visible without a heavy feed. Short-lived stories, reposts, and shares of partner and community content keep you active between the posts that matter, without chasing the algorithm.

  4. 04

    Campaigns & reporting

    Focused content for programs, events, and launches as they happen. Each month you get a one-hour strategy call and a metrics report showing what is working.

  5. +

    Website support add-on

    Website updates are coordinated through Salara and completed by a trusted development partner, so your site stays in step with the rest of your presence. Scoped and billed as a separate line item.
